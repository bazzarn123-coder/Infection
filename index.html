Classic Ataxx / 7th Guest Strategy Game

How to Play Infection

Objective: Dominate the 7x7 board by having the most pieces when the board fills up or no moves remain.

1. Clone (1 Step): Move your piece to an adjacent empty square (orthogonally or diagonally). This clones your piece, leaving the original behind.

2. Jump (2 Steps): Move your piece two squares away. This jumps your piece, vacating its original spot.

3. Infection: Whenever you land on a target square, all adjacent enemy pieces in the surrounding 3x3 area are infected and converted to your color!

Game Over

Player 1 wins with 28 pieces!
